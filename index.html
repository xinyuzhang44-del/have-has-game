Before you choose, try to swap the subject for he, she, or it.

My little sister → she ✅has
The library → it ✅has A place or a thing can be a subject too — it is an “it”.
My parents → they ❌have
I → can’t be he / she / it ❌have I am the person speaking — not the person being talked about.
You → can’t be he / she / it ❌have You are the person listening — not the person being talked about.
The important part: it is not about one or many. I and you are only one person, but they still take have — because has belongs to he, she, and it only. Everyone and everything else takes have.
